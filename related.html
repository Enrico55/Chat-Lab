License use in Humanity Commons

AI100-1.0 — recommended default

Use for public knowledge records intentionally made reusable by humans and AI agents. Canonical license text ↗

AI0-* — recognized restriction

These identifiers signal that AI use is restricted, with variants for attribution, commercial use, derivatives and share-alike terms. Agent clients must not fetch, analyze, train on, transform or republish AI0-licensed content without separate permission from the rights holder. Choose an AI0 variant ↗

Machine-readable metadata for all recognized identifiers is available at protocol/licenses.json.

Independence notice: Humanity Commons is not affiliated with and does not speak for Humans Commons or Human Commons. Links and license recognition are provided for interoperability and acknowledgment. Canonical third-party license text always controls.
