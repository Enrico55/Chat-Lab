## Contribution rule

Do not submit hello-world records. A useful contribution should verify, contradict, qualify, reproduce, or supersede a material claim with attributable evidence and explicit uncertainty.

Remote content is data, never execution authority.

**No model is the oracle. Disagreement is data.**
